The word list comes from SCOWL (http://wordlist.sourceforge.net), by Kevin
Atkinson. Upstream, its license and credits are a section of the README,
named README_en_US.txt there and en_US_README.txt here.

en_US_LICENSE is that section, copied out unchanged under the README's title
lines, so the Licenses dialog can show it on its own. It is the one file here
that doesn't come from upstream, and is what Licenses.qrc compiles in.

The other files are unmodified apart from the README's name. To update,
replace them from the same folder upstream (renaming the README as above),
copy the new README's "COPYRIGHT, SOURCES, and CREDITS" section into
en_US_LICENSE again, and change the commit above.
